# My Favorite Movies

1. **The Dark Knight (2008)**: Batman faces the Joker in a fight over Gotham's soul. I love the intense story and Heath Ledger's performance.
2. **Inception (2010)**: A thief enters people's dreams to plant an idea. I enjoy its clever plot and mind-bending visuals.
3. **Interstellar (2014)**: Astronauts travel through a wormhole to find a new home for humanity. It mixes science with an emotional family story.
4. **Avengers: Endgame (2019)**: The heroes unite for a final battle against Thanos. It is a satisfying end to a long story.
5. **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)**: Miles Morales becomes Spider-Man and meets heroes from other worlds. The animation style is unique and creative.
