# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## Overview

**Stranger Things** is a *science fiction horror* series on Netflix, created by the Duffer Brothers and first released in 2016. It follows a group of friends in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana, who uncover secret experiments and a mysterious parallel dimension.

> A story about friendship, courage, and facing the unknown together.

## Main Characters

1. **Eleven**: a girl with telekinetic powers who escapes a secret lab
2. **Mike Wheeler**: the loyal leader of the friend group
3. **Dustin Henderson**: the funny, clever member of the group

## Why I Love It

- Exciting and mysterious storyline
- Memorable characters
  - Especially Dustin, because he is funny and loyal
  - Eleven's growth across the seasons
- Great 1980s music and atmosphere
